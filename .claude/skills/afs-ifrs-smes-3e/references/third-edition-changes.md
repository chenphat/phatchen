# Third edition (2025) — what changed, and transition from the 2015 edition

> Sources: IFRS for SMEs Accounting Standard (Feb 2025) — Introduction Table 1 "Overview of the amendments"
> (items 1–105), Appendix A paragraphs A1–A50 and Table A1; Basis for Conclusions (BC) on the third edition
> (BC40–BC71 and BC1.x–BC35.x, plus BC Appendix Table A2); IFRS Foundation slides "Overview of the third
> edition". "T1-n" = item n in the Standard's Introduction Table 1.

## The big picture

- Issued February 2025; effective for annual periods beginning on or after **1 January 2027**; earlier
  application permitted (A1; BC60–BC63).
- The slides name the six main changes: Section 2 (aligned with the 2018 Conceptual Framework), Section 9
  (IFRS 10 control model), Section 11 (now "Financial Instruments", old Sections 11 and 12 merged), new
  Section 12 (Fair Value Measurement, from IFRS 13), Section 19 (aligned with IFRS 3 (2008)), Section 23
  (rewritten on IFRS 15, now "Revenue from Contracts with Customers").
- The IASB expects **typical SMEs to make limited changes**; the revised revenue requirements are the main
  implementation effort (BC61–BC62).
- **Not changed** in this edition, on purpose: incurred-loss impairment model kept, no IFRS 9 expected credit
  loss model (BC11.19–BC11.22); IFRS 16 not adopted, the operating/finance lease model stays (BC56); IFRS 14 not
  adopted (BC55); all development costs still expensed (BC18.2–BC18.5); all borrowing costs still expensed
  (BC25.4–BC25.5); no revaluation model for intangibles (BC18.9); no new cost/fair value policy choice for
  investment property (BC16.3); IFRS 18 and IFRS 19 are left for a future
  review (BC3.1 footnote; Table A2).
- The option to use IAS 39 recognition and measurement has been **removed**, so the Standard is now fully
  stand-alone (BC11.12–BC11.14; T1-26).
- Simpler wording in revised Sections 2, 12, 19 and 23 can occasionally give a different answer from full
  IFRS. The IASB accepts this (BC58, BC23.4–BC23.6).

## Effective date (A1)

- Apply the amended and revised sections for **annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027**.
- Earlier application is permitted. **If applied early, disclose that fact** (A1).
- For a calendar-year SME the first third-edition statements are for the year ending 31 December 2027, with
  2026 comparatives. Early adopters could apply it in 2025 or 2026 (module 35 footnote 1).

## General transition rule (A2–A3)

- **A2:** apply the amended and revised sections **retrospectively under Section 10**, except as stated in
  A3–A49.
- **Date of initial application** (used in A4–A49) = the **beginning of the reporting period in which the
  entity first applies the third edition**. For a calendar-2027 adopter this is 1 January 2027, not the start
  of the comparative period (A2).
- **A3 disclosure relief:**
  - the 10.13(b) current-period line-item adjustment amounts need not be disclosed. BC65 explains that this
    would otherwise require parallel accounting systems;
  - if comparatives are not restated under Appendix A, the prior-period line-item adjustment amounts under
    10.13(b)–(c) need not be disclosed either.
- No other general reliefs were added (BC66). Where full IFRS offered a choice between retrospective and
  prospective, the Standard gives SMEs only the simpler (usually prospective) route (BC70).
- **Section 35 vs Appendix A:** Appendix A is for entities already on the IFRS for SMEs Accounting Standard
  moving from the 2015 edition. A first-time adopter applies Section 35 instead, and uses Appendix A only via
  35.10(o) for revenue (BC35.3).

## Impact triage for a typical trading or service SME

The IASB expects limited change for typical SMEs (BC61). Sort the changes by likely effect:

| Likely to affect almost every SME | Affects some SMEs | Rarely relevant |
|---|---|---|
| Section 23 revenue re-mapping and transition choice (A27) | Section 9 control reassessment (groups) | IAS 39 option users (A11) |
| Material accounting policy information (3.17(e), 8.4–8.6) | Section 19 for new acquisitions | Bearer plants (A49), E&E assets (34.11G) |
| 7.19A financing-liability reconciliation | Section 12 for entities with investment property, biological assets or FVTPL investments | Share-based payment amendments (A37–A41) |
| 11.43 ageing and 11.43A maturity tables | Section 21 nil-consideration intragroup guarantees | Lack of exchangeability (A45), if USD/KHR stays exchangeable |
| 6.6 proposed dividends note | 29.34A–29.34D uncertain tax treatments | Section 28 DB changes (only entities with DB plans) |
| 33.7A KMP management-entity fees (if used) | 30.8A advance-consideration rate (foreign-currency deposits paid or received) | Supplier finance (7.19B–C) where none exists |

Points that apply to the USD functional currency, KHR presentation case:

- Section 30 translation procedures (30.17–30.19) did not change. The new paragraphs are 30.5A (lack of
  exchangeability) and 30.8A (advance consideration) (T1-88, T1-89).
- A45(b) puts any lack-of-exchangeability effect on a presentation currency into cumulative translation
  differences, not retained earnings.
- 7.19A(c) requires the debt reconciliation to show the effect of changes in foreign exchange rates as a
  separate reconciling item.

## Reviewer checklist for a 2027 (first third-edition) set

- The basis-of-preparation note cites the third edition. If adopted early, A1 requires disclosure of that fact.
- Revenue: retrospective or prospective choice stated. If prospective, the A34–A36 legacy-contract disclosures
  are given. If retrospective, the A28 expedients used are disclosed (A30).
- New notes present: debt reconciliation (7.19A), supplier finance (7.19B–C), receivable ageing (11.43),
  liability maturity (11.43A), proposed dividends (6.6), KMP management entity (33.7A), Section 12 fair value
  disclosures (current year only, A13).
- Policies rewritten as **material** accounting policy information (3.17(e), 8.4–8.6).
- Fair value measurements apply Section 12 prospectively. Comparatives are not re-measured (A12).
- Business combinations before 2027 are untouched (A22). New ones expense acquisition costs and fair-value
  contingent consideration.
- Change disclosures use the A3 relief correctly. The current-period line-item effects (10.13(b)) are not
  needed.
