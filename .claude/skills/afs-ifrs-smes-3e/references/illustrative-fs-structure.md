# IASB Illustrative Financial Statements (third edition, Feb 2025): structure and wording patterns

> Source: "Illustrative Financial Statements for the IFRS for SMEs Accounting Standard" (Feb 2025), which
> accompanies but is not part of the Standard (paragraphs F1–F5 and the XYZ Group statements). Standard
> paragraphs are cited where the illustrations refer to them. All amounts are in CU as in the source.

## What the illustration is (and is not)

- The example is **XYZ Group**: XYZ (Holdings) Limited, incorporated in "A Land", and its wholly owned subsidiary
  XYZ (Trading) Limited. The group manufactures and sells candles. These are consolidated statements for the year ended
  31 December 20X2 with 20X1 comparatives.
- It shows how Sections 3–8 and the disclosure paragraphs of other sections *might* be met by a typical SME.
  It is **not a template for all entities**. Each entity decides content, order, format and line-item
  descriptions to achieve fair presentation (F1).
- It does not illustrate every requirement. The educational modules carry further section-by-section
  illustrations (F4).
- Titles of statements may differ from those used, if not misleading (F2; 3.22).
- **Third-edition features shown:** debt reconciliation (note 27, 7.19A), receivable ageing (note 11, 11.43),
  liability maturity analysis (note 20, 11.43A), refund liability and returns asset (revised Section 23), and
  revenue recognised on transfer of control.

## The primary statements, in order

1. **Consolidated statement of comprehensive income and retained earnings**, two alternatives:
   - Alternative 1: expenses by **function**;
   - Alternative 2: expenses by **nature** (5.11).
2. **Consolidated statement of financial position** at 31 December 20X2, with **three columns**: 20X2, 20X1 and
   an opening 20X0 column.
3. **Consolidated statement of cash flows**, indirect method.
4. **Accounting policies and explanatory notes** (notes 1–31).

No separate statement of changes in equity is presented. The combined statement replaces both the SoCI and
the SoCE (F3; 3.18).

### Statement of income and retained earnings (3.18, 6.4–6.5)

- The combined statement is allowed because the only equity changes in the periods presented come from profit
  or loss and dividends. 3.18 also allows prior-period error corrections and policy changes (F3).
- No OCI exists, so the statement "could have been titled 'Statement of income and retained earnings'" (F3).
- It ends: Profit for the year → Retained earnings at start of year → Dividends → Retained earnings at end of
  year. 20X2: 381,725; 2,166,150; (150,000); 2,397,875.
- Each version carries a footnote explaining the classification basis and why a single statement was elected.
  Copy this pattern.
- **For a USD functional currency, KHR presentation entity:** translation differences under 30.18(c) are
  recognised in OCI and accumulated in a component of equity. Equity then changes for something other than
  profit, dividends, errors and policy changes, so the 3.18 combined statement is normally not available. Use
  a statement of comprehensive income plus a statement of changes in equity. (This inference follows from
  3.18 and 30.18; the illustration itself has no OCI.)

### Alternative 1 — by function (line items, with 20X2 amounts)

Revenue (note 5) 6,846,037; Cost of sales (5,162,249); **Gross profit** 1,683,788; Other income (6) 88,850;
Distribution costs (175,550); Administrative expenses (810,230); Other expenses (106,763); Finance costs (7)
(28,120); **Profit before tax** (8) 651,975; Income tax expense (9) (270,250); **Profit for the year** 381,725
(20X1: 262,385). A notes column sits beside the line items, and gross profit is the only operating subtotal.

### Alternative 2 — by nature (only the operating lines differ)

Revenue; Other income; Changes in inventories of finished goods, work in progress **and returns asset** (+6,416);
Raw material and consumables used (4,786,699); Employee salaries and benefits (936,142); Depreciation and
amortisation expense (277,060); Impairment of property, plant and equipment (30,000); Other expenses
(231,307); Finance costs (28,120). Profit before tax is the same in both versions (651,975).

How the notes adapt: under function, notes say where items sit (warranty and PPE impairment "included in cost
of sales", notes 8 and 14; software amortisation "included in administrative expenses", note 15). Footnotes
give the by-nature wording instead. Under nature, a separate impairment line on the face makes the note
cross-reference unnecessary (note 14 footnote).

### Statement of financial position

- Order: **current assets first, then non-current; current liabilities, then non-current liabilities, then
  equity**, most liquid first. The reverse order is also permitted (F2).
- Assets: Cash; Trade and other receivables (10); Inventories (12), then a subtotal. Investment in associate (13);
  PPE (14); Intangible assets (15); Deferred tax asset (16), then a subtotal. Total assets 3,432,894.
- Current liabilities: Bank overdraft (17); Trade and other payables (18); Interest payable; Current tax
  liability; Provision for warranty obligations (21); Current portion of employee benefit obligations (22);
  Current portion of obligations under finance leases (23).
- Non-current liabilities: Bank loan (17); Long-term employee benefit obligations (22); Obligations under
  finance leases (23). Total liabilities 1,005,019.
- Equity: Share capital (25) 30,000; Retained earnings (4) 2,397,875. Total liabilities and equity 3,432,894.
- Retained earnings cross-refers to **note 4 (dividend restriction)**, not to a reserves note.
- **Opening column (20X0, shaded):** not required by the Standard. It is included only to help readers
  follow the cash flow calculations (F5 and the footnote to the statement). A third SoFP is not required even
  after restatement (BC10.3). Omit it unless a user needs it.

### Statement of cash flows (indirect method)

- **Operating:** starts from **profit for the year** (not profit before tax). It adds back non-cash finance
  costs (800), **non-cash income tax expense** (79,934), depreciation (275,360), impairment (30,000) and
  amortisation (1,700). It deducts the gain on sale of equipment (63,850), labelled "cash flow included in
  investing activities". Working-capital changes include the warranty provision and employee benefit
  movements. Net cash from operating activities: 701,867.
- **Investing:** proceeds from sale of equipment 100,000; purchases of equipment (485,000). Additions per note
  14 are 545,000 including 60,000 of new finance leases (note 27), so non-cash additions are excluded.
- **Financing:** payment of finance lease liabilities (24,130); repayment of borrowings (100,000); dividends
  paid (150,000).
- Cash and cash equivalents **net of overdraft**: closing (50,695) (note 26: cash 32,905 less overdraft
  83,600).
- Interest and tax paid appear as **footnotes to the face**: finance costs paid in cash (27,320) and income
  taxes paid in cash (190,316). A third footnote says payables include an unrealised FX loss (1,000).

Ordering logic (8.4): first the compliance statement and policies; then supporting notes in the order of
the statements and their line items (income statement items, then SoFP assets, then liabilities, equity, and
cash); last the other disclosures (contingencies, events after the reporting period, related parties,
approval).

## Accounting policy wording patterns (note 2), paraphrased

- **Compliance and currency:** "prepared in accordance with the IFRS for SMEs Accounting Standard issued by
  the IASB", "presented in the currency units (CU) of A Land". For KHR presentation, add the 30.26
  statement of functional currency (USD) and the reason for the different presentation currency.
- **Consolidation:** includes the company and its subsidiary; all intragroup transactions, balances, income
  and expenses eliminated.
- **Associates:** cost less accumulated impairment. Dividend income recognised when the right to receive is
  established, inflow probable and amount reliably measurable; included in other income.
- **Revenue (revised Section 23 wording):** goods recognised when **control transfers**, on delivery.
  Royalties are a percentage of the licensee's sales, recognised as those sales occur. Measured at the
  consideration the group **expects to be entitled to**, net of sales taxes collected for government and
  after considering **rights of return**.
- **Borrowing costs:** all expensed in the period incurred.
- **Income tax:** current tax plus deferred tax; temporary differences defined; DTA only to the extent
  probable; reviewed each reporting date; enacted or substantively enacted rates.
- **PPE:** cost less accumulated depreciation and impairment; straight-line to residual value; rates by class
  (buildings 2%, fixtures and equipment 10%–30%). Revised prospectively **if there is an indication of a
  significant change** (no annual review, BC17.2).
- **Intangibles:** purchased software, cost less amortisation and impairment, five years straight-line; same
  "indication of significant change" wording.
- **Impairment:** at each reporting date, review PPE, intangibles and associates for indicators; compare
  recoverable amount. Inventories compared with selling price less costs to complete and sell. Reversals
  capped at the amount had no impairment been recognised.
- **Leases:** finance versus operating by transfer of risks and rewards. Finance leases at the lower of fair
  value and PV of minimum lease payments, constant periodic rate. Operating lease rentals straight-line.
- **Inventories:** lower of cost and selling price less costs to complete and sell; FIFO. A **returns asset**
  is included, measured at former carrying amount less expected recovery costs and value reductions.
- **Trade receivables:** normal 30-day terms, non-interest-bearing, measured at cost. Amortised cost (EIR)
  only where credit beyond one year. Impairment when **objective evidence** exists (incurred-loss model).
- **Trade payables:** non-interest-bearing. Foreign-currency payables at the closing rate; FX gains or
  losses in other income or other expenses. **Refund liability** = consideration expected to be refunded
  for returns (30-day full refund).
- **Bank loans and overdrafts:** interest by the effective interest method, in finance costs.
- **Long-service payments:** government-mandated, unfunded. Projected unit credit method, salary growth 4%,
  turnover from experience, discounted at high-quality corporate bond yields.
- **Warranty:** one-year defect warranty; provision for estimated cost recognised when revenue is recognised.

## Note wording patterns worth reusing

- **Estimation uncertainty (note 3):** name the balance, cross-refer to its note, list the assumptions
  (salary increases, discount rate, leavers; historical return rates). No numbers are given in the note.
- **Tax reconciliation (note 9):** narrative only. State the rate and explain the difference
  (non-deductible items with amounts for both years). A numerical reconciliation is not used.
- **Ageing (note 11):** "analyses the age … by reference to their due date". Columns: not yet due, <1 month,
  1–3 months, 3 months–1 year, 1–5 years, >5 years. Rows: amortised cost before impairment, impairment, net.
- **Maturity (note 20):** "based on their remaining contractual maturities … contractual undiscounted cash
  flows, including interest payments". The bank loan shows 55,000 undiscounted against a 50,000 carrying
  amount.
- **Deferred tax (note 16):** say why temporary differences on investments are insignificant; explain each
  DTA source; state management's probability judgement; give the movement table by source; explain offsetting
  (same jurisdiction, law allows net settlement) and show gross DTL/DTA.
- **PPE (note 14):** roll-forward for the current year, plus the opening cost/accumulated depreciation of the
  comparative year. Impairment narrative (decline in efficiency led to a review, CU30,000 loss). Finance-lease
  assets included (CU95,000). Directors' resolution to dispose of a machine, with no impairment because
  proceeds are expected to exceed the net carrying amount.
- **Provisions (note 21):** opening, additional accrual, utilised, closing. Reason for current classification.
- **Contingent liability (note 28):** nature of the claim and amount, counsel's view, why no provision.
- **Events after (note 29):** non-adjusting flood with estimated cost and insurance recovery. Dividend
  declared after year-end with per-share amount, payment date and record date, and the statement that no
  liability is recognised because the obligation arose after year-end.
- **Related parties (note 30):** eliminated intragroup transactions mentioned; sales to associate and
  receivable balance (both years); shareholder guarantee at no charge; **total KMP remuneration** as one
  figure (33.7).
- **Approval (note 31):** "approved by the board of directors and authorised for issue on [date]".

## Gaps to fill when adapting the illustration

- No OCI, no translation reserve, no presentation-currency translation. For a USD to KHR entity add the 30.26
  currency disclosures, the 30.25(a)–(b) exchange difference amounts, a statement of changes in equity and a
  translation reserve column.
- No new Section 12 fair value note, because the group has no fair value measurements. Add one if the entity
  has investment property, FVTPL investments or revaluations.
- No supplier finance (7.19B–C), proposed-dividend 6.6 note beyond note 29, or 8.6 judgements note.
  Consider each against the entity's facts.
- The illustration uses CU with no rounding statement. 3.23(d)–(e) require the presentation currency and the
  level of rounding (for example, KHR'000) to be displayed prominently.
