> Sources: IFRS for SMEs Accounting Standard, third edition (2025), Sections 3–8 and the disclosure paragraphs of
> Sections 10–35; Appendix A. Requirements are summarised: read the paragraph before marking a row complete.

## 1. Complete set and identification (Section 3)

- Fair presentation (3.2); explicit and unreserved statement of compliance in the notes (3.3) — only if every
  requirement is met.
- Going concern assessment covering at least twelve months from the reporting date (3.8); disclose material
  uncertainties, or the basis used if not a going concern (3.9).
- At least annual; a longer or shorter period needs the 3.10 disclosures.
- Consistency of presentation (3.11); reclassified comparatives need nature, amount and reason (3.12), or why
  impracticable (3.13).
- Comparative information for all amounts, and narrative where relevant (3.14); so at least two of each statement and
  related notes (3.20).
- Complete set (3.17): SoFP; SoCI (single statement) or income statement + SoCI; SoCE; SoCF; notes comprising
  material accounting policy information and other explanatory information.
- Statement of income and retained earnings permitted in place of SoCI and SoCE only if the only equity changes are
  profit or loss, dividends, error corrections and policy changes (3.18, 6.4). A share issue, a revaluation or a
  presentation-currency translation difference rules it out.
- Identification (3.23): entity name and any change; individual or group; reporting date and period; **presentation
  currency**; level of rounding. Repeat on every page of each statement.
- Domicile, legal form, country of incorporation, registered office; nature of operations and principal activities (3.24).

## 2. Statement of financial position (Section 4)

- Minimum line items 4.2 (a)–(r): cash and cash equivalents; trade and other receivables; other financial assets;
  inventories; PPE (incl. bearer plants); investment property at cost / at FVTPL; intangible assets; biological assets
  at cost / at FVTPL; investments in associates; investments in jointly controlled entities; trade and other payables;
  other financial liabilities; current tax assets and liabilities; deferred tax assets and liabilities (always
  non-current); provisions; non-controlling interest; equity attributable to owners of the parent.
- Additional line items, headings and subtotals when relevant (4.3).
- Current / non-current classification unless a liquidity presentation is more relevant (4.4–4.8).
- Sub-classifications in the SoFP or notes (4.11): PPE classes; receivables from related parties and others;
  inventories (held for sale / WIP / materials); payables to suppliers, related parties, deferred income, accruals;
  employee-benefit provisions and other provisions; classes of equity.
- Share capital disclosures (4.12): shares authorised; issued and fully paid / not fully paid; par value; reconciliation
  of shares outstanding (current period only); rights, preferences, restrictions; own shares held; shares reserved;
  description of each reserve.
- Binding sale agreement for a major disposal (4.14).

## 3. Statement of comprehensive income (Section 5)

- Single statement or two statements (5.2). Minimum line items (5.5): revenue; finance costs; share of profit of
  equity-accounted associates and JCEs; tax expense; single amount for discontinued operations; profit or loss; each
  OCI item by nature, grouped into **will not be reclassified** (translation of a foreign operation, actuarial gains
  and losses, revaluation surplus) and **will be reclassified** (cash-flow hedges); share of OCI of associates; total
  comprehensive income.
- Allocation to NCI and owners of the parent (5.6).
- Additional line items and subtotals when relevant (5.9).
- Analysis of expenses by nature or by function, whichever is reliable and more relevant (5.11). Under the function
  method, cost of sales is shown separately at a minimum.
- Note for this skill: the **presentation-currency translation difference** is an OCI item that is never reclassified
  (30.18(c)) — present it in the "will not be reclassified" group.

## 4. Statement of changes in equity (Section 6)

- 6.3: total comprehensive income (owners / NCI); effects of retrospective application or restatement for each
  component; reconciliation of each component separately disclosing profit or loss, OCI, and transactions with owners
  (issues of shares, treasury shares, dividends, changes in ownership interests).
- 6.6: dividends proposed or declared before authorisation but not recognised, and per share; cumulative preference
  dividends not recognised.
- For KHR presentation, add the **foreign currency translation reserve** column.

## 5. Statement of cash flows (Section 7)

- Operating, investing, financing (7.3); indirect or direct method for operating activities (7.7–7.8).
- Interest and dividends received and paid presented separately and classified consistently (7.14–7.16); income tax
  separately, normally operating (7.17).
- Exclude non-cash investing and financing transactions; disclose them (7.18–7.19).
- **7.19A** reconciliation of liabilities arising from financing activities (financing cash flows, changes from
  obtaining/losing control, FX effects, fair value changes, other).
- **7.19C** supplier finance arrangements (new in the third edition).
- Components of cash and cash equivalents reconciled to the SoFP (7.20); restricted balances with management
  commentary (7.21).
- **7.13**: effect of exchange-rate changes on cash presented separately — always needed with a KHR presentation.

## 6. Notes: structure and order (Section 8)

8.2: basis of preparation and specific policies; information required by the Standard not presented elsewhere;
other relevant information. 8.3: systematic and cross-referenced. 8.4 normal order:
1. statement of compliance with the IFRS for SMEs Accounting Standard;
2. material accounting policy information (8.5) — material if, considered with other information, it can reasonably
   be expected to influence users' decisions;
3. supporting information for items in the order of the statements and line items;
4. other disclosures.
Also: judgements apart from estimates (8.6) and key sources of estimation uncertainty with nature and carrying amount
(8.7). See `illustrative-fs-structure.md` for the IASB's illustrative note order.

## 7. Materiality and aggregation (3.15A–3.16)

- 3.16: information is material if omitting, misstating **or obscuring** it could reasonably be expected to influence
  primary users' decisions; it depends on nature, magnitude or both, individually or in combination.
- 3.15A: decide how to aggregate, including in the notes; do not obscure material information with immaterial
  information or aggregate items of different nature or function.
- Appendix A, A4: the amended materiality requirements apply prospectively from the date of initial application.
- Practical approach for compilation: set an overall materiality from a benchmark (e.g. profit before tax, revenue,
  total assets, equity), a lower performance materiality for line items, and a clearly-trivial threshold; then assess
  every line by size **and** by nature (related parties, covenants, items that turn a profit into a loss). Document
  the benchmark rationale. The Standard sets no percentages — they are professional judgement.

## 10. Report of the Board of Directors (outside the Standard)

The IFRS for SMEs Accounting Standard does not require a directors' report; it is required by company law or
regulation in many jurisdictions. A typical structure (confirm against the applicable law): the company and its
principal activities; financial results (both currencies if useful); dividends; share capital; reserves and
provisions; bad and doubtful receivables; current assets; contingent and other liabilities; going concern; events
after the reporting period; directors who served; directors' interests and benefits; auditors; directors'
responsibilities for the financial statements; approval. Figures must agree to the statements.
