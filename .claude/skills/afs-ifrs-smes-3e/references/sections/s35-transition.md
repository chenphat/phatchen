# Section 35 — Transition to the IFRS for SMEs Accounting Standard (third edition)

> Sources: Educational Module 35 (third edition); IFRS for SMEs Accounting Standard (2025) paragraphs 35.1–35.15
> and Appendix A (A1–A3, A27–A36). Section 30 paragraphs are cited for the currency section. Module examples
> use CU and ignore income tax (module footnote 2).

## When this section matters in an SME compilation

- The entity's first annual financial statements with an **explicit and unreserved statement of compliance**
  with the Standard (35.4). The previous framework might be local GAAP, a tax basis, full IFRS, or no financial
  statements at all.
- The entity used the Standard before, stopped (for example, it moved to local GAAP after a change of owner),
  and is now resuming. Section 35 is then optional (35.2).
- Every opening balance of the earliest comparative period is converted. Equity adjustments go to retained
  earnings (35.8).
- The notes must carry the transition explanation and the equity and profit reconciliations (35.12–35.13).
- **Not** for an entity already on the 2015 edition moving to the third edition. That entity applies
  Appendix A (see the box below).

> **Box: first-time adoption (Section 35) vs moving from the 2015 to the third edition (Appendix A)**
>
> | | Section 35 first-time adopter | Existing SME moving 2015 → 2025 edition |
> |---|---|---|
> | Who | First FS with an explicit, unreserved compliance statement (35.3–35.4) | Already compliant with the 2015 edition |
> | Key date | **Date of transition** = beginning of the earliest period with full comparatives (35.6), e.g. 1 Jan 2026 for a 2027 first set | **Date of initial application** = beginning of the period in which the third edition is first applied (A2), e.g. 1 Jan 2027 |
> | Basic rule | Restate everything at the transition date as if always applied (35.7), adjustments to retained earnings (35.8) | Retrospective under Section 10 (A2) |
> | Reliefs | Mandatory exceptions 35.9(a)–(g); optional exemptions 35.10(a)–(o); impracticability 35.11 | Topic-specific reliefs A4–A49, many prospective (e.g. Section 12 A12, Section 19 A21) |
> | Revenue | 35.10(o): Section 23 retrospective or prospective per A27, reading "date of initial application" as "date of transition"; A31 disclosure not required | A27–A36 directly |
> | Disclosure | Reconciliations of equity (two dates) and profit (35.13) | Section 10 change disclosures with A3 relief (no current-period line-item amounts) |
>
> Effective date: annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027, earlier application permitted and
> disclosed (A1). A first-time adopter applies the version of the Standard effective for its first reporting
> period (module introduction).

## What changed in the third edition

- New **mandatory exception 35.9(g)**: contracts with customers completed before the date of transition are
  not restated. A completed contract is one where all goods or services identified under previous GAAP have
  been transferred.
- New **optional exemption 35.10(o)**: a first-time adopter may apply revised Section 23 retrospectively or
  prospectively, following A27.
- 35.1–35.4, 35.6–35.7, 35.9–35.10 and 35.12A were amended (Table A1). BC35.3: the IASB added no other
  exemptions for third-edition changes because the impracticability exemption is available.

## Scope and key definitions

- **First-time adopter:** an entity presenting its first annual FS that conform to the Standard, whatever its
  previous framework (35.1, 35.4). Examples of when FS are the first such FS (35.4):
  - no FS were presented for previous periods;
  - the last FS followed national requirements not consistent with the Standard in all respects;
  - the last FS followed full IFRS.
- **No partial compliance.** An entity cannot pick and choose between the Standard and full IFRS. FS that
  depart from it (for example, by capitalising R&D) carry no compliance statement and are not first FS
  (module notes; test question 1).
- **Local GAAP "equal to IFRS for SMEs except in name"** with compliance stated only with local GAAP: not a
  first-time adopter. If dual compliance is stated, the entity *is* one (test question 2).
- **Re-adopters (35.2):** an entity whose last annual FS lacked the compliance statement either applies Section
  35 or applies the Standard retrospectively under Section 10 as if it never stopped. Either way it gives the
  35.12A disclosures.
- **Complete set (35.5, 3.17):** SoFP, SoCI (one or two statements), SoCE, SoCF and notes, with comparatives
  (3.14). A published opening SoFP alone is not a first set of FS (Ex 4). Pro forma statements without
  comparatives or a compliance statement do not count either (Ex 2, Ex 5).
- **Date of transition (35.6):** the beginning of the earliest period for which full comparative information
  under the Standard is presented.
  - One comparative year → 1 Jan 20X3 for 20X4 FS (Ex 6).
  - Two comparative years, voluntary or required by law → 1 Jan 20X2 (Ex 6, Ex 7).
- **Impracticable:** cannot apply after every reasonable effort. It is a higher hurdle than undue cost or
  effort; a valuer's fee does not make something impracticable (module notes to 35.11).

## Recognition

At the date of transition, except as 35.9–35.11 allow (35.7):

- **(a) Recognise** all assets and liabilities the Standard requires. Examples:
  - goodwill written off under local GAAP (Ex 8);
  - finance leases previously treated as operating (Ex 9);
  - constructive-obligation provisions (Ex 10);
  - unfunded pension liabilities (Ex 55, case study 2).
- **(b) Derecognise** items the Standard does not permit:
  - capitalised research and development (Ex 11; 18.14);
  - contingent liabilities booked as liabilities (Ex 12);
  - restructuring provisions that fail Section 21 (case study 2).
- **(c) Reclassify**:
  - inventories shown as non-current become current (Ex 13);
  - redeemable preference shares become liabilities at amortised cost (Ex 14);
  - convertible debt is split (Ex 43).
- **(d) Measure** all recognised items under the Standard:
  - revalued intangibles go back to cost (Ex 15);
  - LIFO is replaced by FIFO or weighted average (Ex 16).
- The **opening SoFP is prepared but not presented** in the FS (35.6 notes, 35.7 notes). This differs from
  IFRS 1.

## Measurement

### Initial

- Opening balances are measured as if the Standard had always applied (35.7(d)). Adjustments for events
  before the transition date go **directly to retained earnings**, or another equity category if appropriate
  or required by law (35.8, module footnote 3).
- Adjustments arising between the transition date and the first reporting date go to profit or loss of the
  period in which they arise (35.8 notes). The comparative year is fully restated (for example, goodwill
  amortisation for 20X3, Ex 8).
- **Deemed cost options** at transition (35.10(c), (d), (da)):

| Option | Applies to | Measure | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 35.10(c) fair value as deemed cost | PPE, investment property, intangibles; item by item | Fair value at the transition date | Not available for goodwill (test Q7). Fair value under Section 12 still requires estimates (module) |
| 35.10(d) revaluation as deemed cost | Same assets | A previous GAAP revaluation at or before transition, as deemed cost at the revaluation date | A cost carried without depreciation is not a revaluation (Ex 36) |
| 35.10(da) event-driven fair value | Some or all assets and liabilities | Fair value at an event date (e.g. valuation for a planned sale) | Before transition: deemed cost at that date. After transition but within the periods presented: deemed cost when the event occurs, with the adjustment in equity at that date |
| 35.10(f) separate FS | Investments in subsidiaries, associates, JCEs at cost | Section 9 cost, or deemed cost = fair value or previous GAAP carrying amount at transition | No relief if the fair value or equity method policy is chosen (Ex 41) |
| 35.10(l) decommissioning | Dismantling component of PPE cost | Measure the liability at transition under Section 21 and add the same amount to PPE | Avoids reconstructing the history |
| 35.10(m) rate regulation | PPE and intangibles used in rate-regulated operations | Previous GAAP carrying amount; impairment test under Section 27 | Item by item |
| 35.10(j) extractive (full cost) | Oil and gas assets | Previous GAAP amount, allocated pro rata (reserve volumes or values), impairment test | Ex 44 |
| 35.10(n) severe hyperinflation | All assets and liabilities held before the normalisation date | Fair value at transition; comparative period may be shorter than 12 months | Ex 49–51 |

### Subsequent

- After transition, the normal sections apply. Depreciation runs from deemed cost (Ex 38: 30 June 20X2 fair
  value less depreciation to 31 December 20X2). Goodwill kept under 35.10(a) is impairment-tested at
  transition and amortised under Section 19 afterwards (Ex 30).
- **Mandatory exceptions (35.9).** Previous GAAP accounting is not retrospectively changed for the following
  (mandatory; they cannot be extended to other items):

| Para | Topic | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | Derecognition of financial instruments | Items derecognised under previous GAAP before transition are not re-recognised. Items the Standard would have derecognised may be derecognised at transition or kept until settled (and then impairment-tested or fair-valued) |
| (b) | Hedge accounting | No change for hedges ended before transition. Existing hedges follow Part II of Section 11 from transition, which needs designation and documentation by that date |
| (c) | Accounting estimates | No hindsight. Transition estimates stay consistent with previous GAAP estimates for the same date. New information is treated like a non-adjusting event (32.6–32.7); errors are corrected (35.14) |
| (d) | Discontinued operations | No reclassification or remeasurement of previous GAAP discontinued operations. Applied prospectively from transition |
| (e) | NCI | 5.6 profit/TCI allocation to NCI is prospective from transition (or from the earliest restated business combination) |
| (f) | Government loans | Keep the previous GAAP carrying amount at transition. Do not recognise the below-market benefit as a grant. Apply Sections 11 and 24 prospectively |
| (g) | Completed customer contracts (new) | Contracts completed before transition are not restated |

- **Optional exemptions (35.10)**, in addition to the deemed cost items above. Use one or more; they cannot
  be extended by analogy (module notes):
  - **(a) business combinations:** Section 19 need not be applied to combinations before transition. Restating
    any one requires restating all later ones (Ex 30, test Q8–Q9);
  - **(b) share-based payment:** not required for equity instruments granted before transition, or for
    liabilities settled before transition;
  - **(e) cumulative translation differences:** deemed zero for all foreign operations ("fresh start");
  - **(g) compound instruments:** no split if the liability component is not outstanding at transition;
  - **(h) deferred tax:** Section 29 prospectively from transition, but the 29.3A Pillar Two exception
    retrospectively;
  - **(i) service concessions:** 34.12–34.16 need not be applied to arrangements entered into before
    transition;
  - **(k) lease assessment:** whether an arrangement contains a lease may be judged on facts at transition;
  - **(o) revenue:** retrospective with the A28 expedients, or prospective (only contracts beginning after
    transition use revised Section 23, and contracts in progress keep the old policy).
- **Impracticability (35.11):** if a 35.7 adjustment is impracticable at transition, make it in the earliest
  practicable period and identify the unrestated amounts. Disclose any impracticable omitted disclosure.

## Key judgements and estimates

- **Is the entity a first-time adopter?** Use the module's Figure 1 decision tree: is there a compliance
  statement, were earlier FS presented, and what was the last framework? Example: dual local GAAP and IFRS for
  SMEs compliance means it is a first-time adopter (test Q2).
- **How many comparative years?** This fixes the transition date (Ex 6–7).
- **Which exemptions to use.** Using more exemptions reduces judgement. Examples: fair values at old
  acquisition dates (35.10(a)), retrospective share-based payment valuations (35.10(b)), and decommissioning
  liabilities at origination (35.10(l)) can all be avoided. But exemptions do not remove judgement: fair value
  as deemed cost still needs Section 12 estimates (module, significant estimates).
- **Estimate versus error at transition.** Information available at the time but ignored is an error, so the
  balance is restated and the correction shown separately (Ex 22). Later information is not hindsight-adjusted
  (Ex 21).
- **Impracticability.** Only when records cannot be obtained after every reasonable effort, for example
  records destroyed by fire (Ex 53–54). Cost alone never qualifies.

## Presentation

- The first FS present the current year and restated comparatives as if the Standard had always applied,
  subject to the exceptions and exemptions (35.7 notes).
- No opening SoFP is presented (module comparison with full IFRS).
- Transition adjustments sit in opening retained earnings of the comparative year (or another equity
  component). Hedging gains not previously recognised go to a **hedging reserve** (case study 2: CU431).
- Unrestated comparatives (35.11) must be identified, with an explanation of the lack of comparability.

## Worked examples from the module

1. **Estimates without hindsight (Ex 21–23).** Restoration provision CU9,500 at 31 Dec 20X2 and CU10,000 at
   31 Dec 20X3 under local GAAP. New technology in late 20X4 cuts it to CU5,200.
   - At transition keep CU9,500, with CU10,000 for the 20X3 comparative. The fall goes to 20X4 profit.
   - If the information was available at end-20X2 and ignored (an error, Ex 22): restate to CU4,750 at
     transition and CU5,000 at end-20X3 (CU250 charge in 20X3), then CU200 charge in 20X4.
   - If no provision was booked before (Ex 23): recognise CU9,500 at transition, not CU4,750.
2. **Business combination (Ex 31–32).** Entity A bought B on 1 Jan 20X2 for CU10,000. B's net assets had a
   carrying amount of CU5,000 and a fair value of CU8,000; the CU3,000 difference is machinery with a 10-year
   life. Goodwill is amortised over 5 years.
   - With 35.10(a): goodwill stays at the local GAAP CU4,000 at 1 Jan 20X3 (CU3,000 at end-20X3, CU2,000 at
     end-20X4), with CU1,000 amortisation a year.
   - Without it: goodwill CU1,600 and PPE +CU2,700, so retained earnings rise by CU300. Afterwards amortisation
     is CU400 a year and extra depreciation CU300 a year.
3. **Compound instrument (Ex 43).** CU50,000 4% five-year convertible issued 1 Jan 20X1; market rate 6%.
   Liability component CU45,788 (PV of principal CU37,363 + interest CU8,425); equity CU4,212. At transition
   (1 Jan 20X3) the liability is CU47,327 (−CU2,673), equity−conversion right +CU4,212 and retained earnings
   −CU1,539. The exemption in 35.10(g) is unavailable because the liability is still outstanding.
4. **Government loan (Ex 28).** Loan carried at CU100,000; CU103,030 repayable 31 Dec 20X5. Keep CU100,000
   at transition with no grant. Effective interest charges: CU1,000 (20X3), CU1,010 (20X4), CU1,020 (20X5).
5. **Reconciliation (case study 2, transition 1 Jan 20X4).** Local GAAP equity CU6,560 becomes CU8,075.
   - Adjustments: PPE residual values +80; intangibles to goodwill 150 (reclassification only); FVTPL assets
     +420; production overheads in inventory +400; hedging reserve +431; pension −66; restructuring provision
     reversed +250.
   - Retained earnings +1,084; hedging reserve +431.
   - 20X4 profit: local GAAP CU422 becomes CU586 (+164). Movements: revenue +391 (hedge recycling); cost of
     sales −97; distribution −10; administrative −300 (pension 50 + restructuring 250); other income +180
     (fair value gains).

Other module cases: decommissioning (Ex 47) PPE CU19,210 at transition ((20,000 × 18/20) + 1,210), with
depreciation CU1,067 a year. Case study 1 (LIFO to FIFO): inventory CU400 becomes CU1,000 at 1 Jan 20X2 and
CU2,200 becomes CU3,500 at 31 Dec 20X2; 20X2 cost of sales falls by CU700.

## Review points and common errors

- Wrong transition date. It is the start of the **earliest comparative period**, not the start of the
  current year (test Q3–Q5).
- Comparative year not restated (for example, missing 20X3 goodwill amortisation or LIFO cost-of-sales
  effects), or the opening SoFP not rolled forward into the comparatives.
- Exceptions treated as optional, or exemptions applied by analogy to other items (35.9 and 35.10 notes).
- Hindsight used in transition estimates. Error corrections not separated in the reconciliations (35.14).
- 35.10(a) misapplied: restating an older combination but not later ones. Fair value used as deemed cost for
  goodwill.
- Unexplained "deemed cost". An undepreciated historical cost is neither fair value nor a revaluation (Ex 36).
  A class-wide cost or revaluation policy is still required (17.15).
- Reconciliations missing the second equity date (end of the last previous GAAP period), or reconciling total
  comprehensive income instead of profit.
- Disclosures omitted on the claim of impracticability. The hurdle is high (35.13 notes).
- Revenue: 35.9(g) completed contracts restated, or the prospective route chosen without the legacy-contract
  disclosures.
- An entity already on the 2015 edition wrongly applying Section 35 instead of Appendix A.

## USD functional currency / KHR presentation currency

- **Adjust first, translate second.** Make all Section 35 adjustments in the USD functional-currency ledger,
  then translate each SoFP presented (transition date, end of comparative year, reporting date) at its own
  closing rate (30.18(a)). The opening SoFP is not presented but still needs translating to roll forward
  equity.
- Comparative and current income and expenses are translated at transaction-date rates, or an average if
  rates do not fluctuate significantly (30.18(b), 30.19). Translation differences go to OCI and a separate
  component of equity, never recycled (30.18(c)). See `../usd-khr-translation.md`.
- **Translation reserve at the transition date.** 35.10(e) lets cumulative translation differences for *foreign
  operations* be deemed zero. Whether a single entity may use it for its own USD to KHR presentation-currency
  differences: **source silent — confirm.** If it is not used, reconstructing the reserve requires historical
  rates for equity. Source silent on the method; confirm against firm policy.
- **Functional currency itself.** If previous GAAP books were kept in KHR but the functional currency under
  Section 30 is USD, 35.7(d) requires measurement as if the Standard (including 30.2–30.5) had always applied.
  How to rebuild historical USD balances is not addressed by Section 35. Source silent; consider the 35.11
  impracticability route and disclose.
- **Reconciliations (35.13).** Previous GAAP equity and profit should be presented in the presentation currency
  alongside the IFRS for SMEs figures. The source does not say how to translate previous GAAP amounts if
  previously reported in another currency; confirm.
- Disclose functional currency (USD), presentation currency (KHR) and the reason for the difference (30.26).
- 35.10(n) (severe hyperinflation) and A45 (lack of exchangeability) apply only if USD/KHR exchangeability or
  hyperinflation is an issue. Neither is expected for USD.
- Government loans, deposits or advances in other currencies follow 30.9 (monetary items at closing rate) once
  carried forward at their previous GAAP amount.

## Differences from full IFRS Accounting Standards

(From the module comparison with IFRS 1, as at February 2025.)

- Simpler language. No opening SoFP presented (IFRS 1 requires one).
- Reconciles **profit or loss**; IFRS 1 reconciles total comprehensive income.
- Section 35 has an **impracticability** exemption for adjustments and disclosures; IFRS 1 has none.
- 35.9(d) discontinued operations exception is not in IFRS 1. 35.9(g) completed contracts is an *optional*
  exemption in IFRS 1 but a mandatory exception here.
- Exemptions differ in detail. IFRS 1 is often stricter. 35.10(h) (Section 29 prospectively) has no IFRS 1
  equivalent.
- IFRS 1 has exemptions not relevant here: embedded derivatives, impairment of financial assets, insurance,
  employee benefits, subsidiaries' and associates' assets and liabilities, designation of financial
  instruments.
- Disclosure mapping: 35.11 and 35.13(a) have no IFRS 1 equivalent. IFRS 1 paragraphs 22, 24(c), 25, 27A and
  29–33 have no Section 35 equivalent.
