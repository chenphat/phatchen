# Expected results: worked example

Furniture maker, one month, MNT thousand. Budgeted volume 1,000 chairs, actual 1,100.

## Headline

Operating income was **20,975** against a static budget of **19,500**: **1,475 F**.
That small favourable gap hides two large opposing effects:

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Sales-volume variance (100 extra chairs) | 9,250 F |
| Flexible-budget variance (price and cost control) | 7,775 U |
| **Static-budget variance** | **1,475 F** |

At 1,100 chairs the business should have earned 28,750 (flexible budget); it earned 20,975.

## Subtotals

| | Static | Flexible | Actual | Volume var | Flexible-budget var |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | 250,000 | 275,000 | 269,500 | 25,000 F | 5,500 U |
| COGS | 175,000 | 189,500 | 192,650 | 14,500 U | 3,150 U |
| Gross profit | 75,000 | 85,500 | 76,850 | 10,500 F | 8,650 U |
| Operating expenses | 55,500 | 56,750 | 55,875 | 1,250 U | 875 F |
| **Operating income** | **19,500** | **28,750** | **20,975** | **9,250 F** | **7,775 U** |

## Material flexible-budget variances (≥ 2,000 or ≥ 10 %)

1. **Sales revenue: 5,500 U (−2.0 %).** Average price 245 vs 250 budgeted per chair
   (269,500 ÷ 1,100). Ask: were discounts given to win the extra volume?
2. **Direct materials: 2,200 U (+2.5 %).** 82.0 per chair vs 80.0 standard. Split into
   price and usage variances if quantities are available. Ask: supplier price rise,
   or waste/scrap?
3. **Marketing: 1,200 F (−12.0 %).** Under-spent a fixed budget. Ask: genuine saving or
   a timing difference (campaign moved to next month)? Under-spending while cutting
   prices may be worth discussing.

## Other observations (not material)

- Direct labour 1,100 F and sales commissions 275 F: good control.
- Variable overhead 550 U, fixed manufacturing overhead 1,500 U (5 %), rent and
  utilities 600 U (7.5 %): watch if the trend continues.
- Administrative salaries exactly on budget.

## Review checks

All PASS: line variances reconcile; fixed lines have zero volume variance;
operating income variance = 9,250 F − 7,775 U = 1,475 F.
