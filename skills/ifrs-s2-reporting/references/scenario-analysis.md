# Climate resilience and scenario analysis (S2.22, B1–B18)

## Principles
- **Proportionate, not a fixed method.** Approach commensurate with exposure and with skills,
  capabilities and resources (S2.22, B1–B8). Start qualitative if capacity is low; move to quantitative
  as capability grows and once resources allow (B17).
- **Timing.** Scenario analysis updated at least in line with the strategic planning cycle (e.g. every
  3–5 years); the resilience assessment is updated every reporting period (B18).
- **Use reasonable and supportable information without undue cost or effort** (B8–B10).

## Quality ladder (S&P CSA 2025 categories mapped to IFRS S2)
| Level | Description | IFRS S2 position |
|---|---|---|
| 0 No assessment | Risks not identified | Fails S2.10–12 and 22 |
| 1 Assessment, no scenarios | Risks listed, no scenario analysis | Fails S2.22 |
| 2 Single-dimension | Physical only or transition only | Fails diverse-range expectation of 22(b)(i) unless justified |
| 3 Limited | ≥1 physical and ≥1 transition scenario, any temperature | Can meet S2.22 if commensurate (B1–B8); qualitative allowed (B17) |
| 4 Comprehensive | Both risk types, each with one scenario >2°C and one <2°C | Strongest evidence for 22(b)(i)(4) Paris alignment; expected over time for high-exposure, well-resourced entities |
Context: ~40% of 11,955 companies in S&P's 2025 CSA reported no climate risk assessment; ~50% of
financials none — absence is common, not a reason to delay.

## Quality checklist (apply to SA1–SA9)
1. Physical and transition risks covered.
2. ≥1 scenario below 2°C (Paris-aligned) and ≥1 above 2°C per risk type.
3. Named recognised sources (NGFS, IPCC SSP-RCP, IEA) and why relevant (22(b)(i)(1),(5)).
4. Scope: own operations, upstream suppliers, downstream customers/clients (22(b)(i)(7)).
5. Horizons stated and consistent with the SRRO register (22(b)(i)(6)).
6. Key assumptions: policy (NDC, carbon pricing), macro, local variables (Mekong floods, heat), energy
   mix, technology (22(b)(ii)).
7. Results translated into financial effects and strategy implications (22(a)(i); 15–21).
8. Capacity to adjust: financial headroom, asset redeployment, planned investments (22(a)(iii)).
9. Significant uncertainties named (22(a)(ii)).
10. Date of analysis and next update stated; resilience reassessed annually (22(b)(iii); B18).

## SA1–SA9 working-paper structure
| WP | Content |
|---|---|
| SA1 | Purpose, scope, proportionality assessment (exposure vs capability → qualitative/quantitative) |
| SA2 | Scenario selection: name, source, temperature outcome, risk type, rationale |
| SA3 | Time horizons and operational scope (own ops / upstream / downstream; sites, portfolios) |
| SA4 | Key assumptions by category (policy, macro, local physical variables, energy, technology) |
| SA5 | Physical risk analysis: hazard × exposure × vulnerability per site/asset/portfolio |
| SA6 | Transition risk analysis: carbon price (CR-06 Cambodia ETS scenarios), demand shifts, technology costs, buyer requirements |
| SA7 | Financial effects: P&L, balance sheet, cash flow ranges per scenario and horizon; FS link |
| SA8 | Resilience conclusion and capacity to adjust |
| SA9 | Uncertainties, limitations, quality-ladder rating, two actions to move up a level, next update date |

## Translating results into financial effects (SA7)
| Driver | Example (Cambodia) | Financial line |
|---|---|---|
| Carbon price × emissions | Buyer/EU CBAM-style cost pass-through on garments | Cost of sales, margin |
| Demand shift | Brands require supplier decarbonisation; loss of orders | Revenue |
| Physical damage | Flood at Phnom Penh SEZ; Tonle Sap flooding of paddy | PP&E impairment, insurance, inventory |
| Productivity | Heat stress reduces sewing output | Labour cost, revenue |
| Supply disruption | Hydropower shortfall → grid outages → diesel backup | Energy cost, Scope 1 |
| Credit risk (banks) | Drought → borrower income ↓ → PD ↑; flood → collateral value ↓ → LGD ↑ | ECL (IFRS 9) |
| Asset lives | Coal-linked or brick-kiln assets stranded earlier | Depreciation, impairment |
Climate-adjusted expected loss: EL = PD × LGD × EAD, with PD and LGD shocked per scenario.

## Scenario sources
- **NGFS** (central banks): Net Zero 2050 (orderly, ~1.5°C), Below 2°C, Delayed Transition, Current
  Policies (~3°C), NDCs. Good for transition and macro-financial variables; used by banks.
- **IPCC SSP-RCP**: SSP1-2.6 (<2°C), SSP2-4.5, SSP5-8.5 for physical hazards.
- **IEA**: NZE, APS, STEPS for energy and technology pathways.
- Cambodian context: NDC 3.0 (up to 55% by 2035, conditional), LTS 2050; Mekong hydrology and flood
  data; Ministry of Water Resources and Meteorology.

## Common errors
Using "a scenario" without stating temperature path or source; physical-only analysis for an exporter
exposed to buyer decarbonisation; results not converted to money or strategy; horizons inconsistent with
the risk register; no capacity-to-adjust discussion; no update date.
