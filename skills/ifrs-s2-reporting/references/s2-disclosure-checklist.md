# IFRS S2 disclosure checklist and paragraph map

Basis: IFRS S2 (June 2023) and *Amendments to Greenhouse Gas Emissions Disclosures* (11 December 2025,
effective for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027, earlier application permitted).
IFRS S1 general requirements also apply (boundary, materiality, connectivity, comparatives, judgements,
compliance statement) — see the ifrs-s1-reporting checklist. Status: Y / P / N / NA / R (relief).

## 7. December 2025 amendments (effective 1 January 2027; early application permitted)
Targeted reliefs and clarifications for GHG measurement and disclosure (confirm wording against the
amendment text before final use):
1. **Scope 3 Category 15 measurement** — an entity may limit Cat 15 disclosure to financed emissions
   (excluding, e.g., emissions associated with derivatives, facilitated emissions and insurance-associated
   emissions), with disclosure of the amount of the excluded items.
2. **Industry classification for financed emissions** — relief from using GICS for disaggregation;
   another classification system may be used (explain which).
3. **Jurisdictional relief for measurement method** — where a jurisdiction or exchange requires a method
   other than the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard for all or part of emissions, that method may be used
   for that part.
4. **GWP jurisdictional relief** — GWP values required by a jurisdiction may be used instead of the latest
   IPCC assessment. Absent a Cambodian requirement, use AR6.
Disclose the reliefs used.

## 9. Common review findings
1. Single combined Scope 1+2 total; Scope 2 basis not stated (29(a)(i),(v)).
2. Calculator AR4 GWPs reported unchanged (B21–B22).
3. Scope 3 limited to business travel without screening rationale for other categories (29(a)(vi)).
4. Banks: no financed emissions or PCAF data quality, despite Cat 15 being the dominant category.
5. Scenario analysis absent or single-dimension (physical only) without explanation (22).
6. Targets without base year, gross/net, or credit reliance (33–36).
7. Restated prior-year emissions without disclosing reason and effect (S1.83–86, B52).
8. Amounts for 29(b)–(g) omitted with no explanation.
