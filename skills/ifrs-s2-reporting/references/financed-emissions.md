# Financed emissions (Scope 3 Category 15) for banks, MFIs, asset managers and insurers

Anchors: S2.29(a)(vi)(2), B58–B63 (asset management, commercial banking, insurance); December 2025
amendments; PCAF Global GHG Accounting and Reporting Standard (2nd ed., 2022); GHG Protocol Scope 3 Cat 15.

## What IFRS S2 asks of a commercial bank (summary)
- Absolute gross financed emissions disaggregated by Scope 1, 2 and 3 of counterparties, by industry and
  by asset class.
- Gross exposure to each industry by asset class (amount and % of total, incl. funded and undrawn
  loan commitments where specified).
- Methodology, including the allocation (attribution) method relative to exposure size.
- Percentage of gross exposure included in the financed emissions calculation; explain exclusions.
- Dec 2025 amendments: may exclude derivatives, facilitated emissions and insurance-associated emissions
  (disclose excluded amounts); may use an industry classification other than GICS (state which).
Confirm the exact sub-requirements against B58–B63 and the amendment text before drafting.

## PCAF attribution
Financed emissions = Σ attribution factor × counterparty emissions.
| Asset class | Attribution factor |
|---|---|
| Listed equity and corporate bonds | Outstanding amount ÷ EVIC |
| Business loans and unlisted equity (private company) | Outstanding amount ÷ (total equity + total debt) |
| Project finance | Outstanding amount ÷ (total project equity + debt) |
| Commercial real estate | Outstanding amount ÷ property value at origination |
| Mortgages | Outstanding amount ÷ property value at origination |
| Motor vehicle loans | Outstanding amount ÷ vehicle value at origination |

## PCAF data quality score (1 best – 5 worst)
| Score | Basis |
|---|---|
| 1 | Verified reported emissions of the counterparty |
| 2 | Unverified reported emissions, or physical-activity data with primary energy data |
| 3 | Physical activity data with emission factors (e.g. production volumes) |
| 4 | Economic activity: revenue × sector emission factor |
| 5 | Economic activity: asset/outstanding amount × sector emission factor |
Report the exposure-weighted average score per asset class; a plan to improve it is good practice.

## Cambodian bank / MFI considerations
- Portfolio dominated by SME, agriculture, construction and housing loans: most counterparties do not
  report emissions → scores 4–5 initially (revenue- or asset-based sector factors).
- High-emitting sectors to prioritise: brick kilns, cement and construction, rice milling (biomass),
  rubber and cassava (land-use change), transport, coal-linked lending.
- Agriculture: paddy methane (CH4 non-fossil AR6 = 27.0) and land-use change dominate.
- Link to credit risk: climate-adjusted PD/LGD for flood/drought-exposed agriculture books (Case C:
  EL = PD × LGD × EAD); disclose in S2.15–21 financial effects and S1.21–23 connectivity with IFRS 9 ECL.
- Observed gap: ACLEDA's 2025 report discloses Scope 3 only for air travel; an ISSB-compliant report
  (after the year-1 relief) needs Cat 15.

## Procedure (FE-01)
1. Extract loan book at period end by asset class, sector (classification stated), outstanding and
   undrawn amounts.
2. Screen scope: in/out (derivatives, facilitated, insurance-associated under the amendments), record % of
   exposure covered.
3. Obtain counterparty data (reported emissions, financials: equity + debt, revenue; property values).
4. Compute attribution factor; apply counterparty Scope 1+2 (and Scope 3 where required/available).
5. Assign PCAF score per exposure; compute weighted score.
6. Aggregate by sector and asset class; reconcile exposures to the GL/FS loan note.
7. Review: outliers, sector factor sources/years, double counting across asset classes.
