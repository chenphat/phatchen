# Cambodia context for ISSB reporting

Status as understood at November 2026 — confirm every item marked ☐ before relying on it for a client.

## Regulators and frameworks
| Body | Relevance |
|---|---|
| ACAR (Accounting and Auditing Regulator) / NAA | Adopts accounting standards (CIFRS = full IFRS; CIFRS for SMEs) and would adopt IFRS S1/S2 and ISSA 5000. ☐ Formal adoption instrument and effective date — treat ISSB application as voluntary unless confirmed. |
| SERC (Securities and Exchange Regulator of Cambodia) / CSX | **Prakas N.034 F.S.A.PK (10 May 2024) on Sustainability Disclosure**: listed entities disclose ESG information from 2026 against 31 specified items. ☐ Whether SERC will reference IFRS S1/S2 directly. |
| NBC (National Bank of Cambodia) | Governance and risk Prakas for banks/MFIs; Cambodian Sustainable Finance Principles. ☐ Any climate-risk or ISSB requirement for banks. |
| KICPAA | Professional body; CPD recognition. |
| Ministry of Environment | NDC 3.0 (Aug 2025: up to 55% reduction by 2035, conditional), Long-Term Strategy for Carbon Neutrality 2050, grid emission factor (see IFRS S2 skill). |

IFRS S1.8: ISSB standards can be applied with CIFRS, CIFRS for SMEs or any GAAP — no conversion needed.

## SERC Prakas 034 — 31 items mapped to ISSB
A Cambodian listed entity can use its Prakas 034 report as the starting point for an S1/S2 gap analysis.
The Prakas mirrors the TCFD/ISSB four pillars for climate, environment and social risks (items 3–8) and
adds impact metrics (items 9–31) that under ISSB are disclosed only if they are material information
about an SRRO (or required by IFRS S2 — e.g. Scope 1 and 2).

| Prakas item | Topic | Closest ISSB requirement | Typical gap vs ISSB |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Business model | S1.32; S2.13 | Value chain concentration not shown |
| 2 | SDGs in strategy/governance | (no direct requirement) | SDG mapping ≠ financial materiality |
| 3 | Key climate, environmental and social risks, short/medium/long term | S1.30; S2.10 | Horizons not defined or linked to planning; opportunities missing |
| 4 | Risk management processes | S1.43–44; S2.24–25 | Integration with ERM, scenario use not described |
| 5 | Strategy to address risks | S1.33; S2.14 | No financial effects (S1.34–40), no resilience/scenario analysis (S1.41, S2.22) |
| 6 | Board governance of risk | S1.26–27; S2.5–7 | Skills, frequency, remuneration link |
| 7 | Key metrics | S1.45–50; S2.29, S2.32 | Cross-industry metrics (b)–(g) and SASB industry metrics missing |
| 8 | Targets and performance | S1.51; S2.33–36 | Base period, milestones, gross/net, carbon credits |
| 9–18 | Training, injuries, child/forced labour, complaints, anti-corruption, whistleblowing, gender (board/management/workforce), Indigenous peoples and communities | S1 (if material to an SRRO); SASB topics | Link to SRRO and financial effect not made |
| 19–20, 25–26 | Environmental and climate policies | S1.33; S2.14 | Policy ≠ transition plan with resourcing and progress |
| 21–24 | Water withdrawn, wastewater, solid waste and recycling | S1 metrics if material; SASB | Data quality and boundary not stated |
| 27–29 | Energy consumed, renewable share, energy produced | S2.29 / industry metrics | Units and boundary |
| 30–31 | Scope 1 and Scope 2 GHG | S2.29(a) | **Scope 3 (incl. Cat 15 financed emissions for banks), location-based Scope 2 basis, GHG Protocol method and GWP basis, consolidation approach** |

Observed practice (ACLEDA Bank Plc. Sustainability Report 2025, voluntary early Prakas alignment):
Scope 1 3,155 tCO2e and Scope 2 21,100 tCO2e (group); Scope 3 only Cat 6 air travel (bank only);
Scope 2 factor switched to the Ministry of Environment grid factor with 2023 restated; no external
assurance; no scenario analysis or financed emissions disclosed. These are exactly the gaps an ISSB
gap analysis for a Cambodian bank should prioritise (S2.22, S2.29(a)(vi) and B58–B63, S1.83–86 for the
restatement disclosure, ISSA 5000 readiness).

## Cambodian SRRO prompts by sector
- **Garment/footwear exporters**: buyer decarbonisation and EU due-diligence rules; heat stress;
  grid outages; minimum-wage and labour compliance; flood risk at SEZ sites; imported fabric Scope 3.
- **Rice, rubber, cassava**: flood/drought yields; paddy methane; land-use change and deforestation-free
  buyer rules; bearer-plant impairment (IAS 16/41); smallholder livelihoods; Indigenous communal land and FPIC.
- **Banks/MFIs**: physical risk in agriculture and construction books; transition risk (brick kilns,
  coal-linked lending); over-indebtedness and client protection; financed emissions; DFI funding conditions.
- **Listed issuers generally**: Prakas 034 compliance; Khmer/English consistency; audit committee oversight.

## Presentation
- Currency consistent with FS (S1.24): KHR or USD; state the translation rate used for any
  illustrative conversions (e.g. USD 1 ≈ KHR 4,000).
- Khmer and English versions must carry identical figures; define terms in both languages.
