# IFRS S1 disclosure checklist and paragraph map

Basis: IFRS S1 (June 2023). Use as the review checklist (WP S1-11). Status codes: **Y** disclosed,
**P** partial, **N** not disclosed, **NA** not applicable (explain), **R** relief applied (state which).
Where a sub-paragraph letter is uncertain, cite the range — never guess a letter.

| Relief | Para | Effect | Disclose |
|---|---|---|---|
| No comparatives | E3 | Comparative information not required in year 1 | Fact relief used |
| Timing | E4 | May report after the FS: with next half-year/Q2 interim report, or within 9 months of year-end (as the paragraph specifies for the entity's interim situation) | Fact relief used |
| Climate-first | E5 | May report only climate-related information (IFRS S2) and apply S1 only as it relates to climate | Fact relief used |
| Year-2 comparatives for climate-first | E6 | In year 2, comparatives required only for climate information | — |

IFRS S2 adds: continue a non-GHG-Protocol measurement method and omit Scope 3 (incl. financed emissions)
in year 1 (S2.C3–C5). See `ifrs-s2-reporting`.

## 7. Common review findings (and why they matter)

1. Materiality based on impacts (GRI) presented as ISSB materiality — fails S1.17–19; users cannot
   see which risks affect prospects.
2. Time horizons stated but not linked to planning/capital-allocation cycles (S1.30(c)).
3. Financial effects described only qualitatively without explaining why (S1.40).
4. No connection to FS judgements: e.g. drought risk disclosed but not mentioned in bearer-plant
   impairment testing (S1.21–23).
5. Targets without base period or interim milestones (S1.51).
6. "Prepared in accordance with ISSB" claimed while Scope 3/resilience omitted without relief — breaches S1.72.
7. Unassured or unsourced metrics with no estimate disclosure (S1.77–82).
8. Prior-period figures changed without explanation (S1.83–86, B52).
